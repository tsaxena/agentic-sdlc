# Stage 1 - Understand

Read `CHALLENGE.md` and `AGENTS.md`.

Apply the `intent-spec` skill to produce `INTENT.md`, including its self-check before presenting.

`INTENT.md` must contain a `## Contract` section holding the acceptance criteria and hard constraints. Every later artifact copies that section verbatim, so write it carefully and keep it testable.

## Boundaries

Do not propose an architecture, choose frameworks or models, design components, or write code.

## Budget

5 minutes. If you hit it, ship `INTENT.md` with unresolved items under `## Open Questions` and say so.

## Human Gate

Present `INTENT.md`, then list separately:

* assumptions you made that a reviewer should confirm
* any ambiguity that genuinely needs a human decision

Stop. Do not start Stage 2 until the user approves.
