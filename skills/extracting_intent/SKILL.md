# Extracting Intent

## Purpose
Convert an ambiguous engineering challenge into a concise problem specification.

## Inputs
- CHALLENGE.md

## Procedure
Extract:
1. Goal
2. Functional requirements
3. Non-functional requirements
4. Constraints
5. Assumptions
6. Acceptance criteria
7. Ambiguities

Separate explicitly stated requirements from inferred assumptions.

Prefer concrete, testable acceptance criteria.

## Guardrails
- Do NOT propose an architecture.
- Do NOT choose agents, frameworks, models, or tools.
- Do NOT start implementation.
- Do NOT invent requirements unless clearly marked as assumptions.
- Keep the output concise enough to review during an interview.

## Output
Create or update INTENT.md.

Target length: ~1 page.
